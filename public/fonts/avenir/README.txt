Optional: IN Groupe's corporate typeface is Avenir (licensed font, NOT bundled in this repository).
If your organisation holds a web-font licence, drop the files here named:
  avenir-roman.woff2   (weight 400)
  avenir-heavy.woff2   (weight 800)
and the dashboard picks them up automatically (see public/css/fonts.css). Locally installed
"Avenir Next" / "Avenir" are used first when present. Fallback: Nunito Sans (SIL OFL), bundled.
